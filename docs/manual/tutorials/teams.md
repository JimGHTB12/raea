raea's Team plan is for people who make things together. This tutorial starts a team, invites a teammate, and shows
what a team shares: its work, its storage and its computers.

## What a team shares

A team on raea shares its projects, its storage and its computers. Everyone reviews the same work and leaves notes on
the same frames, and there's one price for the whole team, with no seats to count.

## Start a team

Open your account on raea.io, and go to Team. Press Get Team to start one, and checkout sets up the plan for everyone.

## Invite your teammates

Under Invite someone, add a teammate by their GitHub login. They see the invite the next time they open their account,
and join with one press. The team's owner can remove members from the same list.

## Review together

In the app, every note says who wrote it, a person or an agent. Teammates review the same feed and pin notes on the same
frames, and each note keeps its author. Each person's agents sign in with that person's own account, so nobody shares a
login.

## Render on everyone's computers

On Team, your teammates' computers render as one. When you render, raea splits the piece into ranges of frames, and sends
each range to a teammate's computer that's online and idle. Each computer renders its range on its own graphics card,
and raea joins the ranges back together, in order. A frame is the same whichever computer draws it, so the result is
identical, only sooner. Each member chooses whether their computer takes part, and when.

## Storage and search, shared

The team's media lives in one shared pool of storage, and everyone searches it together. The next tutorial explains how
that storage works.
